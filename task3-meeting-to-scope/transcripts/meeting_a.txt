[Topic: Initial Discovery & Scope Definition for LocalTrades Directory Platform]

Alex: Understood. Radius search within 25 miles and a faceted sidebar filter widget with category and review filters. What about the layout of the listings?

David: We want a modern card layout—a grid view rather than a long vertical text list. We also want to hide contractor phone numbers from casual anonymous visitors; visitors should create a free account to reveal phone numbers and request quotes.

David: Yes! We will have two membership tiers. Tier 1 is a Free Basic listing where contractors get a basic profile card with their business name, category, and address. Tier 2 is our Premium Gold Plan at $49 per month. For the Gold Plan, contractors get verified badges, direct lead quotes, up to 10 photo uploads, and can post customer coupons. And very importantly, we want to offer a 14-day free trial on the Gold Plan so they can test it before being charged.

Alex: Perfectly clear: Free Basic plan, and $49/month Premium Gold plan with a 14-day trial period. How will you collect those recurring monthly membership fees?

David: We want to use Stripe for credit cards. We have an existing Stripe merchant account. Can Brilliant Directories handle recurring monthly subscriptions automatically through Stripe?

Alex: Yes, Brilliant Directories has a native Stripe Connect gateway integration that handles automated recurring subscriptions, webhooks, and failed payment retries. We will configure that directly. Now, what about job leads—when a homeowner submits a "Get a Free Quote" request?

David: That's our core value proposition! When a homeowner submits a quote request, the system must auto-match the lead based on category and location radius, and route it to up to 3 matched Gold contractors. We also want contractors to receive an instant SMS alert on their phone in addition to email when a lead matches.

Alex: Noted: Auto-matching by category and radius, capped at 3 contractors per lead, with email and SMS notifications. What about branding and visual design?

David: We have our brand guide: primary navy blue (#0f2b5c) and gold accents (#e5a93b). We want all primary search buttons, headers, and footer accents styled in our navy blue. We don't need a totally custom theme from scratch, just clean CSS styling that matches our brand.
